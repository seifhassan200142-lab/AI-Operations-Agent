# Escalation Guide

## High Priority Escalation
Escalate a ticket when it affects an enterprise customer, has a delivery delay over 24 hours, involves damaged goods, or blocks payment on a large order.

## Billing Issues
Billing corrections should include the ticket ID, customer company, requested change, and invoice impact.

## Shipping Issues
Shipping delays should include order number, carrier status, last scan time, and customer impact. If no carrier update exists for more than 24 hours, create an escalation task after approval.
