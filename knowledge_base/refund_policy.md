# Refund Policy

## Standard Refund Window
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of delivery when the product is unused and returned in its original condition.

## Damaged Items
If an item arrives damaged, the support team should collect evidence such as photos, delivery notes, and order details. Damaged-item refund exceptions can be considered outside the normal window after manager review.

## Escalation
Refund requests involving enterprise accounts, delayed shipments, or damaged goods outside the normal refund window should be escalated to operations leadership.
