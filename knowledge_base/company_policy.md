# Company Operations Policy

## Response Time Targets
High-priority customer issues should receive an internal owner within one business hour. Medium-priority issues should be reviewed within one business day. Low-priority issues can be grouped into weekly operational planning.

## Internal Task Ownership
Each operational task must have a clear title, priority, and source context. High-priority tasks should not be created automatically without a human decision-maker approving the escalation.

## Customer Communication
External customer emails should be reviewed by a human before use. Messages must be professional, clear, and avoid promises that the team cannot guarantee.
